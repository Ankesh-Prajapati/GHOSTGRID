About GhostGrid

Attacker coordinates are geolocated from live, publicly aggregated blacklist feeds (real IPs currently flagged across dozens of open threat lists). Target coordinates and attack-vector labels are simulated for visualization, since no free public feed exposes real victim telemetry. This is a portfolio / research project — not an operational SOC tool.

Built by Ankesh Prajapati, Security Analyst & Penetration Tester.
